## Your Task

Generate a Polar MELD that captures BOTH poles of this concept:
- **Positive pole**: The concept expressed accurately, skillfully, appropriately
- **Negative pole**: The concept expressed poorly, distorted, misused, or as a failure mode

For EACH pole, also identify **confusables** - things that look similar but are actually different concepts.

## Important Guidelines

1. **Both poles are "about" this concept** - the negative pole isn't absence, it's corrupted presence
2. **Be honest about failure modes** - include misuse, distortion, hallucination, bias as relevant
3. **Confusables are hard negatives** - things most likely to cause false positives
4. **Source confusables** from nearby concepts when their positives could be mistaken for this concept
